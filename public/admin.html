Request queue

Songs, albums and artists leave the queue by themselves when a matching song is uploaded, and move to Previous requests. For a Spotify playlist, Refresh reads the playlist again and checks which of its songs are already in the library; when all of them are, the playlist is added to the Playlists tab with Spotify's picture and leaves the queue. Open the arrow to see what is still missing.

Advertising

An ad shows at the top of the front page (Recently added › Songs), above the songs. Write it in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, for example a slider with clickable buttons. Only you can change it. It runs in an isolated frame, so it cannot touch the rest of the site or your admin session. Links open in a new tab; add target="_top" to a link to stay in the app. When the ad is off, the page looks as it did before.
